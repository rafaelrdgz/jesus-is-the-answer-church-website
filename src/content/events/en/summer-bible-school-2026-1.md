  Summer Bible School 2026

  Jesus is the Light of the World (John 8:12)


  The Lord granted us a joyful day at our Summer Bible School, under the theme “Jesus is the Light of the World.” Every lesson, activity, and song pointed to the glorious truth that Christ is the only light that dispels the darkness of sin and guides us to eternal life.


  The children listened and learned that Jesus not only illuminates our path, but that He Himself is the way, the truth, and the life. Everything we experienced was an opportunity to exalt Christ and plant in their hearts the sure hope that is found only in Him.


  With sincere gratitude, we thank God for raising up brothers and sisters and volunteers who reflected the love of Christ in their service. Their dedication was a living testimony that when we serve the little ones, we serve the Lord Himself.


  To Christ be all the glory!  May His light continue to shine in our families and in our community, until He comes.
